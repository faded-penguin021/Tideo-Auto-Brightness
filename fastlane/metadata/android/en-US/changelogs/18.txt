Accessibility (TalkBack) improvements, part 1: sliders and switches now announce their labels, the per-field help buttons are properly named instead of reading a bare symbol, section headers are navigable as headings, and read-only value rows are read as one item. Further screens get the same treatment throughout this release.

Diagnostics: Tools now keeps the app's most recent crash report on-device so you can copy it when reporting a problem. Nothing is sent anywhere.
