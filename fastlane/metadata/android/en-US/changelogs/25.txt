Night Light temperature and circadian scaling now work where the display ignores the setting (e.g. OxygenOS), with Shizuku or root.

Fixes:
- circadian Night Light anchoring to 2850 K, not your temperature
- "device default" Night Light drifting warmer each off/on cycle
- backup and restore, broken since 1.8.2
- brightness skipping the first reading at start, ignoring a return to a previous light level and slowing with proximity covered
- the notification reverting to "Monitoring ambient light"
