Fixes settings backup and restore, broken since 1.8.2: the app pointed Android at a backup component that did not exist, so your settings were neither saved to nor recovered from a backup.

Fixes Night Light circadian scaling anchoring its night end to a fixed 2850 K instead of your own temperature, and a temperature left on "device default" turning into a number that drifted warmer on each off/on cycle. Your own value is now put back when the ramp stops driving it.
